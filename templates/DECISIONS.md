## D2 - No user accounts

**Date:** 2026-09-10
**Choice:** Tickets go to email. No login, no passwords.
**Instead of:** Email + password accounts so buyers can see past tickets.
**Because:** Accounts mean password resets, storing personal data, and support
messages at 2am. Buyers already have the email. Same result, none of the work.
**Costs us:** A buyer who deletes the email has to ask the band directly.
Accepted - 10 shows a year, it'll be rare.
